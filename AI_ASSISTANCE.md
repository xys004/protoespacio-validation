# AI Assistance Statement

AI-assisted coding and editorial tools were used in preparing this repository.
Their role included helping draft, refactor, review, and document parts of the
validation modules, pytest tests, build scripts, and manuscript-facing
explanations.

The author reviewed the resulting code and documentation. The author remains
responsible for the scientific claims, the mathematical interpretation of each
validator, the design and acceptance of the tests, and the final manuscript
content.

The validation policy is intentionally auditable: claims are paired with
executable tests, and the complete test suite must pass before the manuscript
build is considered valid.
